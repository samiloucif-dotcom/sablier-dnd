Faces

A locked person does not exist on the players' board — no lane, no placeholder, no count. A lane appears the moment you unlock them.

Places

Unlock a room once they have walked it or been told its name. They see the player-facing label only — your own names, with the floor numbers and the dormitory rosters, never leave the server.

Hand them a clue

Lands in their Ledger marked as yours, in about a second. Whatever face or place you name here is unlocked along with it, so a clue can never arrive mentioning something the board refuses to show.

Your five minutes stay yours. The players' board has no cells and no tick marks below the quarter hour; their spans land on the minute. The mirror below draws your five-minute grid faintly under their lanes — that grid exists only on this page.

Backup & restore

Render's free tier has no disk that survives a restart, and it puts the site to sleep after fifteen quiet minutes. Everything below is how the table's work survives that. Download after every session; the file that lands in your campaign folder is the copy that counts.
